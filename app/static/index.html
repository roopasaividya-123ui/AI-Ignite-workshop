Frequently Asked Questions

How does TruthGuard avoid AI hallucinations?

Unlike conversational chatbots that generate text from ungrounded training data, TruthGuard uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) coupled with a strict abstention mechanism. If no indexed fact-check or official record exists for a claim, TruthGuard returns "Unverified / Insufficient Evidence" rather than guessing.

What does the credibility score represent?

The credibility score (0–100) is an evidence-based assessment indicator derived from supporting versus contradicting authoritative evidence, source transparency, and linguistic manipulation patterns. It is not a mathematically absolute probability that a claim is true.

Can I analyze viral WhatsApp and social media forwards?

Yes! Simply switch to the "Text / Headline" tab in the analyzer and paste the viral message. TruthGuard automatically sanitizes forwarding headers, extracts atomic claims, and scans for phishing or scam domains.
